Earthquakes are natural.
Disasters are not.

Across … years and … earthquakes, the data tells one consistent story: the countries that suffer most are not those with the strongest quakes, but those with the fewest resources to prepare and respond.

GDP per capita, human development, and healthcare infrastructure are stronger predictors of earthquake deaths than magnitude itself. The fault lines that kill are economic, not geological.
